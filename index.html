Welcome to In A Stitch!

In A Stitch is a simple resource for learning or expanding your crochet skills at any level! Whether you’re brand new to the craft or looking to expand your skills, we've collected guides, patterns, and tips to help you grow as a creative.

Ready to get started?

This page features some basic info on the biggest tool you'll need to crochet -- a crochet hook! We'll tell you about different kinds, their benefits and downsides.

Stitches

On this page we discuss the building blocks of this craft: stitches. It features descritptions, images, and helpful Youtube tutorials for you visual learners.

Patterns

Now that you've learned the basics, in the "Patterns" page, we provide a few patterns for any level crafters to get you started! There is a brief descript and links, so just click the picture to get started.
